# Team Palinoia

Welcome to the digital home of **Team Palinoia**! We are the official Classical Dance Team of K.J. Somaiya School of Engineering (KJSSE). Guided by our motto, *"Strive Until Perfection,"* we proudly represent our college at prestigious cultural festivals through the beautiful art of classical Indian dance.

This project is a labor of love, built as part of our Web Programming Lab (WPL) mini-project. It's not just a website; it's a centralized hub designed to make our team's life easier. We built this full-stack application from scratch using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, and MySQL to handle everything from public outreach to our internal day-to-day operations.

## Why We Built This
Managing a vibrant, active dance team comes with its fair share of headaches. Before this system, we struggled with:

* **Keeping track of tasks:** We didn't have a good way to assign and monitor who was doing what (like tracking rehearsal schedules or costume fittings).
* **Managing our roster:** Adding or updating member info meant digging directly into a database—definitely not user-friendly!
* **Connecting with our audience:** People who wanted to reach out or collaborate didn't have a structured way to contact us, and their messages would easily get lost.
* **Sharing our story:** We needed a beautiful, unified place to show the world who we are, what we do, and the performances we're so proud of.

This web app solves all of that by giving our team a proper administrative dashboard while offering visitors a polished, welcoming public face.

## What It Does (Features)
* **Task Management:** Admins can seamlessly assign and track team responsibilities.
* **Member Directory:** A clean interface to manage team members without touching a single line of SQL.
* **Query Handling:** A dedicated contact portal where public messages are safely stored and easy for admins to review.
* **Public Portfolio:** Beautiful pages showcasing our story, past events, and incredible team members.
* **Secure Access:** Role-based login ensuring that only authorized members can tweak internal data.
