Our Story

KJSsE Palinoia is the official classical dance team of K.J. Somaiya School of Engineering. We are a collective of passionate artists dedicated to preserving and exploring the profound depths of Indian classical dance.

Guided by our motto, "Strive Until Perfection," we blend traditional rhythmic complexities and expressive storytelling with exceptional stagecraft. Representing our college at prestigious cultural festivals and events, our mission remains to invoke the divine through movement.
