Our Story

Founded in 2020, Palinoia Dance Ensemble is a collective of professionally trained artists dedicated to preserving and exploring the depths of classical Indian dance forms. Our members bring years of rigorous training from esteemed gurus across the country.

We blend traditional rhythmic complexities and expressive storytelling with contemporary stagecraft. Whether performing at prestigious cultural festivals, international stages, or intimate community events, our mission remains the same: to invoke the divine through movement and inspire our audiences.
